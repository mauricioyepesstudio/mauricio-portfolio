## Enquiry qualification
Ask which service, business goal, existing website/tools, desired timing and budgetrange; never ask for passwords or sensitive customerdata.
Discovery-call output: fit/no-fit, agreed nextstep and written scope. Use existing contact form; do not claim CRM tracking implemented.
## Measurement
Manually record source, service, qualified enquiry, proposal and deposit. No baselines or conversionrates invented. Prefer simple campaign UTM tags after actual source is chosen. Existing form does not prove UTM persistence.
Success gate: real enquiry and owner-verified receipt before paid promotion. No contact email test sent in this delivery.
## Commercial limits
Individual written proposals determine scope, revisions, milestones and price. Third-party software, hosting, ad spend and appstore costs separate. No guaranteedleads, automated dailyincome or unlimitedsupport.
